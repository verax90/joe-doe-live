          SP-404 / MIDI out

          If the SP comes in early or late against the studio, move this until they hit together.

          On the SP: SHIFT + pad 13 → SYSTEM → MIDI tab. MIDI Mode as chosen here; MIDI Sync on USB (or Auto) so it follows the clock, not Internal.

          Looper

          Record a few bars of your voice over the pattern and they repeat in time, layer on layer.

            Sampler

            Take a few bars of what sounds and keep them as your own sounds.

            Backing track

            A song under your patterns: your own file, or the sound of another tab.

            To play over it, match the tempo: tap Tap to the song's beat.

            Pump

            Every kick pushes the rest down for a moment and lets it swell back.
